◆ means

ArrayXXr suzerain::reacting::operator_common_block::means

The mean quantities, stored as collocation point values in means, are as follows:

  • u The nonlinear operator computes the instantaneous spatial (x, z) mean streamwise velocity profile. The linear operator then uses the information to compute the implicit \(f\cdot{}u\) and \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}u}\cdot{}u\) terms in the total energy equation.
  • v Treated identically to u.
  • w Treated identically to u.
  • uu Treated identically to u.
  • uv Treated identically to u.
  • uw Treated identically to u.
  • vv Treated identically to u.
  • vw Treated identically to u.
  • ww Treated identically to u.
  • SrhoE The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}E}\) term in the energy equation.
  • Srhou The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}u}\) term in the streamwise (x) momentum equation.
  • Srhov The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}v}\) term in the wall-normal (y) momentum equation.
  • Srhow The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}w}\) term in the spanwise momentum equation.
  • Srho The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}}\) term in the density equation.
  • Srhou_dot_u The nonlinear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{S}_{\rho{}u}\cdot{}u\) term in the energy equation.
  • fx The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean streamwise (x) component of the implicit \(f\) term in the momentum equation.
  • fy The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean wall-normal (y) component of the implicit \(f\) term in the momentum equation.
  • fz The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean spanwise (z) component of the implicit \(f\) term in the momentum equation.
  • f_dot_u The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(f\cdot{}u\) term in the energy equation.
  • qb The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(q_b\) term in the energy equation.
  • CrhoE The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}E}\) term in the energy equation.
  • C2rhoE The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}E}\).
  • Crhou The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}u}\) term in the streamwise (x) momentum equation.
  • C2rhou The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}u}\).
  • Crhov The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}v}\) term in the wall-normal (y) momentum equation.
  • C2rhov The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}v}\).
  • Crhow The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}w}\) term in the spanwise momentum equation.
  • C2rhow The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}w}\).
  • Crho The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}}\) term in the density equation.
  • C2rho The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}}\).
  • Crhou_dot_u The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the implicit \(\mathscr{C}_{\rho{}u}\cdot{}u\) term in the energy equation.
  • C2rhou_dot_u The linear operator accumulates the time-step-specific temporal mean of the quantity \(\mathscr{C}^2_{\rho{}u}\cdot{}u\).

"Time-step-specific temporal means" are time averages taken across a single time step of quantities which vary on each substep. As the substeps are all of equal length, a simple running mean is reset on substep zero and then accumulated.

Each mean quantity is a single column within means. This facilitates operations across the entire wall-normal profile in a stride one fashion.

See also
writeups/treatment_channel.tex and writeups/derivation.tex for details on the mean quantities. Column-major storage housing all mean quantities (one per column).

◆ refs

ArrayXXr suzerain::reacting::operator_common_block::refs

The reference quantities stored in refs are as follows:

  • ref_ux Reference \(C^{u_x} \)
  • ref_uy Reference \(C^{u_y} \)
  • ref_uz Reference \(C^{u_z} \)
  • ref_Cmy_rho Reference \(C^{my_rho} \)
  • ref_Ce_rho Reference \(C^{e_rho} \)
  • ref_Ce_rv Reference \(C^{e_rv} \)

Each reference quantity is a single row within refs. This facilitates a stride one operation loading or writing all reference quantities for a single wall-normal location.

See also
rholut_imexop.h for details on linearization and the associated reference quantities. Column-major storage housing all mean quantities (one per row).
