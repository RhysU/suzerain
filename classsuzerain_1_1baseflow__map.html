Detailed Description

Provides map-based lookup of arbitrary baseflow profiles established at runtime.

This may be used with, e.g., suzerain_radialflow_solver and suzerain_radialflow_cartesian_conserved to compute baseflow information on the collocation points for a given suzerain::bspline.
