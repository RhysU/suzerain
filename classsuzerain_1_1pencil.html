Detailed Description

template<typename FPT = real_t, typename Allocator = typename blas::allocator<FPT>::type>
class suzerain::pencil< FPT, Allocator >

Encapsulates a pencil-storage-based scalar field to be in-place transformed between physical and from wave space by pencil_grid.

Handles details like maintaining size information, indexing, appropriate storage order. Here physical indicates real-valued storage of pointwise values and wave indicates complex-valued storage of Fourier coefficients.

The \( x \), \( y \), and \( z \) directions are designed to be the streamwise, wall-normal, and spanwise directions respectively. The storage is arranged so that the wall-normal direction is stride one in wave space while the streamwise direction is stride one in physical space.
