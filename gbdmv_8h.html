Detailed Description

Provides custom, BLAS-like diagonal matrix times general band matrix-vector operations.

Includes mixed real/complex operations and fixed bandwidth kernels.

Compute \( y \leftarrow{} \alpha{} D A x + \beta{} y \).

Transposes of \( A \) can be taken using the trans parameter.

Parameters
transOne of 'N', 'T', or 'C' for no transpose, a transpose, or a conjugate transpose, respectively.
nDimension of all matrices and vectors.
klNumber of subdiagonals in band storage of a.
kuNumber of superdiagonals in band storage of a.
alphaMultiplicative scalar \( \alpha \).
dStorage for diagonal matrix \( D \).
lddLeading dimension of d.
aGeneral band storage for matrix \( A \).
ldaLeading dimension of a.
xVector to be multiplied.
incxStride of vector x.
betaMultiplicative scalar \( \beta \).
yVector to be added to product and to contain result.
incyStride of vector y.
Returns
Zero on success and a BLAS-like error code otherwise.

A BLAS reference for more details, especially for general band storage matrix requirements.
