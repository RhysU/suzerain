Detailed Description

Nondimensional boundary layer pressure gradient parameters.

Most of these quantities are defined and discussed within Cal and Castillo's "Similarity analysis of favorable pressure gradient turbulent boundary layers with eventual quasilaminarization." in Physics of Fluids 20 (2008).

One notable exception is \(p_{e,x}^\ast\) which is motivated by the radial nozzle problem implemented in radialflow.h and discussed at length in writeups/radialflow.tex.
