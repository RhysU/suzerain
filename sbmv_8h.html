Detailed Description

Provides custom, BLAS-like symmetric band matrix-vector operations.

Includes mixed real/complex operations and fixed bandwidth kernels.

Parameters

nNumber of rows and columns in matrix a.
kNumber of super-diagonals in band storage of a.
alphaMultiplicative scalar \( \alpha \).

xVector to be multiplied.
