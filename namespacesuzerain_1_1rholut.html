Detailed Description

Provides compute kernels for formulations using a reference density \(\rho_0\), length \(l_0\), velocity \(u_0\), and temperature \(T_0\).

The ideal gas equation of state is employed:

\begin{align*} p &= \left(\gamma-1\right) \left( e - \mbox{Ma}^{2} \frac{m\cdot{}m}{2\rho} \right) \\ T &= \gamma{} \frac{p}{\rho} \\ \mu &= T^{\beta} \\ \lambda &= \left(\alpha - \frac{2}{3}\right) \mu \end{align*}

The following nondimensional variable definitions are used:

  • \(p\) or p is pressure.
  • \(T\) or T is temperature.
  • \(\vec{u}\) or u is the velocity vector.
  • \(\rho\) or rho is density.
  • \(\vec{m}\) or m is the momentum vector \(\rho\vec{u}\).
  • \(e\) or e is the total energy \(\rho\tilde{e}\) where \(\tilde{e}\) is the sum of the internal and kinetic energy densities.
  • \(\mu\) or mu is the first viscosity.
  • \(\lambda\) or lambda is the second viscosity.
  • \(\alpha\) or alpha is the scaling factor relating \(\lambda\) and \( \mu \).
  • \(\beta\) or beta is the constant coefficient in the power viscosity law.
  • \(\gamma\) or gamma is the constant ratio of specific heats \(C_p/C_v\).
  • \(\mbox{Ma}\) or Ma is the Mach number \(u_0/a_0\).

Templated Scalar, Vector, and Tensor types are used. These functions are intended to be used with Eigen's vector and matrix types. For example, when Scalar == double Vector and Tensor may be Eigen::Matrix<Scalar,3,1> and Eigen::Matrix<Scalar,3,3>, respectively.

Many compute kernels in this nondimensionalization are identical to ones used in suzerain::rholt. Those kernels have been brought into this namespace with using declarations.

Compute the explicit portion of \(\vec{\nabla}p\).

Uses the expansion

\begin{align*} \nabla{}p &= \left(\gamma-1\right)\mbox{Ma}^{2}\left( \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}}{\rho^{2}} - \left\{\frac{\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}}{\rho^{2}}\right\}_0 \right)\vec{\nabla}\rho - \left(\vec{\nabla}\vec{m}\right)^{\mathsf{T}}\left( \frac{\vec{m}}{\rho} - \left\{\frac{\vec{m}}{\rho}\right\}_0 \right) \right) \end{align*}

where \( \left\{\frac{\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}}{\rho^{2}}\right\}_{0} \) and \( \left\{\frac{m}{\rho}\right\}_{0} \) are fixed by refcoeff_grad_rho and refcoeff_grad_m, respectively. The remaining linear portion of \(\vec{\nabla}p\) is

\[ \left(\gamma-1\right)\vec{\nabla}e + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}\mbox{Ma}^{2} \left\{\frac{\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}}{\rho^{2}}\right\}_{0} \vec{\nabla}\rho - \left(\gamma-1\right)\mbox{Ma}^{2} \left(\vec{\nabla}\vec{m}\right)^{\mathsf{T}} \left\{\frac{m}{\rho}\right\}_{0} \]

Unlike many other methods beginning with explicit_, when zero reference coefficients are used the full \(\vec{\nabla}p\) term is not recovered.

Uses the expansion

\begin{align*} \vec{\nabla}\cdot\vec{\nabla}p = \left(\gamma-1\right)\left[ \vec{\nabla}\cdot\vec{\nabla}e - \frac{\mbox{Ma}^2}{\rho}\left[ \operatorname{trace}\left( \vec{\nabla}\vec{m}^{\mathsf{T}}\vec{\nabla}\vec{m} \right) + \left(\vec{\nabla}\cdot\vec{\nabla}\vec{m}\right)\cdot\vec{m} - \rho^{-1}\left[ 2\vec{\nabla}\vec{m}^{\mathsf{T}}\vec{m} \cdot\vec{\nabla}\rho + \frac{1}{2}\left(\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}\right) \vec{\nabla}\cdot\vec{\nabla}\rho - \rho^{-1}\left(\vec{m}\cdot\vec{m}\right) \left(\vec{\nabla}\rho\cdot\vec{\nabla}\rho\right) \right] \right] \right] . \end{align*}
