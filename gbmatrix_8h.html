Compute the BLAS-compatible offset to a(i,j) for general banded matrices a(i,j) -> storage(ku+i-j,j) where storage is column-major with leading dimension ld.

When comparing with BLAS documentation, note missing constant one compared to Fortran due to C's 0-indexing.

Compute the starting point and extents of the j-th column within a general banded matrix.

Assuming s == sizeof(double) on entry and the returned value was p, then ((double *)p)[il] is the first nonzero entry in column j and ((double *)p)[iu] is the one past the last nonzero entry of the same column.

Parameters
[in]mNumber of rows.
[in]nNumber of columns.
[in]klNumber of subdiagonals.
[in]kuNumber of superdiagonals.
[in]aBLAS-compatible general banded storage.
[in]ldLeading dimension for column-major ordering.
[in]sSize of each matrix entry in bytes. For example, sizeof(double).
[in]jDesired column in the half-open range [0,n).
[out]ilFirst nonzero entry's row index i in column j.
[out]iuLast plus one nonzero entry's row index i in column j.
Returns
A pointer which, after being cast to match s, may be dereferenced to access entries in column j of a.

Compute the starting point, stride, and extents of the i-th row within a general banded matrix.

Assuming s == sizeof(double) on entry and the returned value was p, then ((double *)p)[jl*inc] is the first nonzero entry in row i and ((double *)p)[ju*inc] is the one past the last nonzero entry of the same row.

Parameters
[in]mNumber of rows.
[in]nNumber of columns.
[in]klNumber of subdiagonals.
[in]kuNumber of superdiagonals.
[in]aBLAS-compatible general banded storage.
[in]ldLeading dimension for column-major ordering.
[in]sSize of each matrix entry in bytes. For example, sizeof(double).
[in]iDesired row in the half-open range [0,m).
[out]jlFirst nonzero entry's column index j in row i.
[out]juLast plus one nonzero entry's column index j in row i.
[out]incIncrement between entries in row i.
Returns
A pointer which, after being cast to match s, may be dereferenced to access entries in row i of a.
