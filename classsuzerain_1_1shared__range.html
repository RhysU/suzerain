Detailed Description

template<class T>
class suzerain::shared_range< T >

A shared_range extends the functionality of iterator_range by adding smart pointer-like resource ownership semantics.

Semantically, shared_range resembles a std::shared_ptr<T[]> which "knows" its own length. Instances assume ownership of pointers provided to constructors and guarantee resources will be deleted when the last shared_range pointing to them is destroyed or reset.
