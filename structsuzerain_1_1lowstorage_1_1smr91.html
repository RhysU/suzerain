Detailed Description

template<typename Component, typename Integer>
struct suzerain::lowstorage::smr91< Component, Integer >

Encapsulates essential constants for the three stage, third order scheme from Appendix A of Spalart, Moser, and Rogers' 1991 `‘Spectral Methods for the Navier-Stokes Equations with One Infinite and Two Periodic Directions’' published in the Journal of Computational Physics volume 96 pages 297-324.
