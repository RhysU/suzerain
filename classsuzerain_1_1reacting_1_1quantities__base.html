Detailed Description

Encapsulate the mean quantities detailed in the "Sampling logistics" section of writeups/perfectgas.tex.

Samples of each quantity are made available through a two-dimensional, column-major arrays. The row index iterates over wall-normal collocation point locations and the column index iterates over tensor indices. Scalars have only a single tensor index. Vector quantities have three indices corresponding to the streamwise x, wall-normal y, and spanwise z directions. Symmetric tensors (for example, \(\overline{\mu{}S}\)}) have six entries corresponding to the xx, xy, xz, yy, yz, and zz indices. Rank one triple products (for example \(\overline{\rho{}u\otimes{}u\otimes{}u}\)) have ten entries corresponding to the xxx, xxy, xxz, xyy, xyz, xzz, yyy, yyz, yzz, and zzz indices.

Many memory overhead and implementation consistency issues have been traded for the headache of reading Boost.Preprocessor-based logic. So it goes.
