Definition common.hpp:189

A Boost.Preprocessor shifted sum operation written by Steven Watanabe which operates on the second element of each tuple in a sequence (http://lists.boost.org/boost-users/2009/10/53245.php).

It, for example, takes the sequence of tuples ((A, 1)) ((B, 1)) ((C, 1)) ((D, 2)) ((E, 4)) to the sequence of tuples ((A, 0)) ((B, 1)) ((C, 2)) ((D, 3)) ((E, 5)) and is handy for computing absolute offsets given a sequence containing names and sizes.
