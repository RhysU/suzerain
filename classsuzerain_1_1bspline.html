Detailed Description

Provides a thin RAII wrapper for a gsl_bspline_workspace.

The wrapper includes convenience methods to simplify working with the GSL API. Non-constant methods are not thread-safe.

Create a B-spline workspace capable of evaluating piecewise polynomials of degree k - 1 on nbreak breakpoints located at breakpoints.

The resulting basis will have nbreak + k - 2 degrees of freedom.

Parameters
kB-spline order per GSL/PPPACK conventions. For example, 4 denotes piecewise cubics.
tagDispatch tag to identify this constructor.
nbreakNumber of breakpoints.
breakptsStrictly increasing breakpoint locations.

gsl_bspline_alloc() and gsl_bspline_knots()

Create B-spline workspace capable of evaluating piecewise polynomials of degree k - 1 on a knot vector created to best approximate the nabscissae provided Greville abscissae.

The resulting basis will have nabscissae degrees of freedom.

Parameters
[in]kB-spline order per GSL/PPPACK conventions. [in] For example, 4 denotes piecewise cubics.
[in]tagDispatch tag to identify this constructor.
[in]nabscissaeNumber of abscissae.
[in]abscissaeStrictly increasing Greville abscissae locations.
[out]abserrAbsolute error over all approximated abscissae when not NULL.
See also
gsl_bspline_alloc() and gsl_bspline_knots_greville() with the latter detailing the best approximation requirements.
