Detailed Description

Compute channel quantities of interest.

Beware that many of these computations neglect covariance-related correction terms that should be present when working with functions of expected values. For example, when computed from mean velocity \(\bar{u}\) and speed of sound \(\bar{a}\),

\[ \operatorname{E}[\mbox{Ma}] \approx \operatorname{E}[u]/E[a] + \operatorname{Var}(a) \operatorname{E}[u] /\operatorname{E}[a]^3 - \operatorname{Cov}[a,u] \operatorname{E}[a]^2 \]

are the appropriate corrections to be second-order accurate.
