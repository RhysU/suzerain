◆ suzerain_filterop_boundary_treatment

enum suzerain_filterop_boundary_treatment

Indicates the chosen filtering behavior at the boundaries.


Enumerator
SUZERAIN_FILTEROP_BOUNDARY_IGNORE 

Apply the interior scheme at all points, including the points near the wall.

Out-of-domain values are treated as if they are zero.

SUZERAIN_FILTEROP_BOUNDARY_NOFILTER 

No filtering is performed at the boundary points nearest the edge of the domain.

SUZERAIN_FILTEROP_BOUNDARY_SYMMETRY 

Reflect near-boundary points outside the domain and apply the interior scheme.

SUZERAIN_FILTEROP_BOUNDARY_PERIODIC 

Enforce periodic conditions across the domain boundaries.
