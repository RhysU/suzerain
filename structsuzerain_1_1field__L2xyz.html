Detailed Description

Holds information on the \(L^2_{xyz}\) norm of a scalar field or inner product of two scalar fields.

To compute root-mean-square (RMS) values instead of \(L^2\) quantities, divide mean2, fluctuating2, and total2 by \(L_x L_y L_z\). To obtain RMS values from mean, fluctuating, and total, divide each of them by \(\sqrt{L_x L_y L_z}\).
