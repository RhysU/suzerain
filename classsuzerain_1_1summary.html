Detailed Description

Summarizes quantity components from suzerain::samples and their derivatives on collocation points.

Instances are meant as a human-readable, B-spline-agnostic data interchange and post-processing contiguous storage format. Accordingly, easy access to names and descriptions are provided.

Many memory overhead and implementation consistency issues have been traded for the headache of reading Boost.Preprocessor-based logic.
