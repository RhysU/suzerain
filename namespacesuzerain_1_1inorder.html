Translate the index range [tb, te) from a length T DFT to a length S DFT.

In-order storage for T != S implies the index range may be broken into at most two contiguous ranges.

More specifically, when ignoring wavenumbers not found in a length S DFT, the data kept in [tb,te) is equivalent to [tb1,te1) and [tb2,te2). Further, the range [tb1,te1) contains data equivalent to [sb1,se1) and [tb2,te2) contains data equivalent to [sb2,se2).

Such information could be used to copy data prepared for DFTs of disparate lengths. Two steps would be necessary. First, a copy from [sb1,se1) to [tb1,te1). Second, a copy from [sb2,se2) to [tb2,te2). Either copy may be of zero length.
